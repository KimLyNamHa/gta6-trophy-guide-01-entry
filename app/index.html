Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

  1. Stage 1 — Story, 60–80 hours. Play Chapters 1–5 in order. Manual save at the start of each chapter: there is no mission replay. Do the Lucia balcony meditation in Chapter 1, after Down to Earth, before the chapter ends — the only possibly missable trophy.
  2. Stage 2 — During the story. Try all 9 activity types, scan vehicles and landmarks with WAiNK, and start stranger strands as they appear. Trophy-related quest givers return later if they go quiet.
  3. Stage 3 — After the story, free roam. Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work it: 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 100 optional tasks, and the collectibles.
  4. Stage 4 — Clean-up. Finish the 8 stranger strands, the count trophies (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 WAiNK, 50 challenges), then the platinum pops.

⚠ Only 1 possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia may disappear after Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether it counts, so do it to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.
