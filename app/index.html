Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price! · 7 stages

  1. Stage 1 — Story Ch. 1, first hours. Play Gone Fishing, then Chapter 1 in order. Manual save at the start of each chapter: there is no mission replay. Do the Lucia balcony meditation after Down to Earth, before Chapter 1 ends — the only possibly missable trophy.
  2. Stage 2 — Story Ch. 2–3, 60–80 hours. Finish the story, try all 9 activity types, scan vehicles and landmarks with WAiNK, study animals with binoculars, and start stranger strands as they appear.
  3. Stage 3 — After the story, free roam. Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work it: 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 100 optional tasks.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles. 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 15 card sets, 100 WAiNK locations, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 Cocaine Flamingos, 18 meditation spots.
  5. Stage 5 — Counts. 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 shooting-range golds, 50 challenges (70 for 100%), 100 vehicles, 500 Criminal Profile actions, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent.
  6. Stage 6 — Strangers & Freaks. Finish the 8 counting stranger strands, then Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — trophy quest givers return later if they go quiet.
  7. Stage 7 — Clean-up & disputes. Finish the 3 unconfirmed and 4 disputed trophies last, checking each against the newest guide; then the platinum pops.

⚠ Missable audit — only 1 possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia may disappear after Chapter 1, and PowerPyx says it is unconfirmed whether it counts, so do it to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story. No other trophy is missable in any source we checked.
